# Classic MLG montage assets

The featured montage uses original meme recordings, **no AI speech**. The
user-supplied Suno song remains the main score. These short, transformed
excerpts are accents rather than replacement copies of the source tracks.

## Soundboard recordings

The community [MLG soundboard mirror](https://github.com/anli5005/mlg-soundboard)
at revision `4950f2ad00387ec97201c12476a4ff6ddd855c56` supplies the airhorn,
Call of Duty hitmarker and sniper effect, “Mom Get the Camera,” “Oh Baby a
Triple,” “Damn Son,” excited gamer reaction, “Get Noscoped,” tournament
“Wombo Combo” commentary, and Eddy Wally “Wow” clips.

The brief music stings are Skrillex's *Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites*
(2 seconds from the soundboard), Skrillex's *Bangarang* (2 seconds from
[this public sound button](https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/bangarang-drop/)),
and Darude's *Sandstorm* (1.5 seconds from the soundboard). The primary score
is the supplied *Announcer breaks the sound*, credited in the media README.

[`manifest.json`](manifest.json) records each direct source URL, original
file hash, exact crop, encoded excerpt hash and graphic provenance. Complete
downloaded soundboard files and full commercial tracks are not included.
The renderer applies bass boost and saturation to the excerpt stems, ducks
the main score for vocal clips and nearly mutes it for classic music stings.
The final mix is mastered separately with a true-peak ceiling.

The mirror does not provide a blanket license for these third-party recordings.
Original recording and music rights remain with their respective owners;
the repository's code license does not relicense the excerpts. Inclusion here
is as brief excerpts in the requested montage parody, not a royalty-free pack.
The repository owner confirmed redistribution permission before public release;
see [NOTICE](../../../NOTICE.md) for the limits of that confirmation.

## Graphics

- Mountain Dew logo: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountain_Dew_logo.svg).
  The PNG is a 500-pixel rasterization of the included SVG. PepsiCo trademark.
- Doritos logo: [Wikipedia file page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Doritos_Logo_(2013).png).
  Frito-Lay / PepsiCo copyright and trademark.

The can and chip bag bodies, hitmarker overlays, speed lines, sunglasses,
camera zooms and meme typography are drawn in the renderer. The brand marks
are used as montage-parody props; no endorsement or sponsorship is claimed.
These third-party graphics are not relicensed under the project code license.

All RF figures and receiver footage come from this project. Decorative
hitmarkers are separate from the eight explicitly labeled PHY-header checks;
the montage makes no claim to decode the two-stream HE payload.
