RTL8922AE PCIe sample extraction — 2026-10-12

Result
The capture reservation was enlarged from 256 KiB to 320 KiB and tested on
beastmode. Three full 327680-byte I/Q snapshots were retrieved. Pluto's
125 kHz baseband tone appears in every 8192-record block, including the extra
64 KiB. Its received frequency is approximately 111.2–111.7 kHz because of
the previously measured receiver/transmitter oscillator offset. No clipping
was observed; the MAC LA data-loss indicator was zero.

The normal PCIe receive DMA infrastructure can also deliver scope reports:
the relocated MAC scope control at 0x11460 produces type-3 reports, which
the temporary driver's instrumented RX path captures. Those reports contain
packet/debug data and timestamps, including recognizable Wi-Fi frame bytes.
They do NOT establish a raw ADC/DFIR I/Q DMA stream. A continuous Pluto tone
did not turn this path into a sustained sample stream.

Ring layout and validation
WDE: 0x00000..0x16000, unchanged 1408 x 64-byte pages.
LA:  0x16000..0x66000, 320 KiB, 40960 eight-byte records.
PLE: 0x66000..0x8f800, 1089 linked + 239 unlinked 128-byte pages.
Firmware tail: 0x8f800..0x90000, unchanged.
Both SCC and DBCC initialization tables use the experimental layout.
PLE cma0_tx and cma1_tx minimum quotas were reduced from 320 to 128 pages
each. Other minimum quotas and reserved firmware quotas were preserved.
Their sum is now 987 linked pages, leaving 102 shared pages. Per-queue maxima
were adjusted accordingly. This is a diagnostic receive layout, not a tested
general Wi-Fi configuration. The card remains unbound outside experiments.
Readbacks: WDE 0x8c08=0x05800000; PLE 0x9008=0x04413301.
LA size selector 4 in 0x9660 selects 320 KiB. Start bound remains 11.
The pointer wrapped at approximately 32.8 ms at nominal 1.25 MS/s, versus
26.2 ms for the previous 256 KiB ring. State remained running until stopped.
This establishes 320 KiB operation, not the largest physically possible ring.

Transfer bottleneck
The verified 32-bit MMIO binary readout previously measured about 2.19 MB/s.
Minimum verified sample production is 1.25 million records/s x 8 = 10 MB/s.
A 320 KiB copy at that readout speed takes approximately 150 ms, while the
ring fills in 32.8 ms. A bigger ring alone cannot sustain lossless reception.
The chip's complete 0x90000-byte shared SRAM is finite and also holds WDE,
PLE and firmware reservations; enlarging it does not change this rate deficit.

Linux x86 memcpy_fromio uses 32-bit transactions. Explicit 64-bit readq was
tested as a possible optimization. It returned all-ones data and failed
byte-for-byte comparison against the existing verified 32-bit dump. Its
apparent 4.3 MB/s is invalid and must not be quoted as sample throughput.
That change was reverted; the saved final patch uses memcpy_fromio.
Reference: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/v6.8/arch/x86/lib/iomem.c

DMA investigation
Instrumentation counts descriptors immediately after parsing their DMA RX
descriptor, counts delivery into core RX, and preserves type-3 scope payloads
with a six-u32 little-endian header: payload length, free-run count,
seq|bb_sel<<16, descriptor packet size, monotonic nanoseconds low/high.
Normal descriptor counters were nonzero (65–74 during bring-up), validating
that the observation path executes. A 16 MiB bounded host buffer stores reports.

Known controls tested:
0x8938 bits11:10=1: dispatcher type-3 forwarding to host.
0x8924 bit3: type-3 RXBD tagging; bit19: CPU enqueue selection.
0x210bc bits28/29: BB scope enable / automatic LA configuration.
0x21094 bits26:25: r_dma_rdrdy choices.
0x9660: normal LA enable/start with correctly reserved ring.

Old vendor scope addresses 0xce60/0xee60 were not suitable for this chip.
The relocated 0x11460 (band 0), 0x15460 (band 1) candidates were derived from
multiple matched neighboring RX-register relocations: CE20->11420,
CE4A->1144A, CE4B->1144B, CE64->11464. This was an inference from source,
then tested on hardware. 0x11460 writes read back and produced scope DMA.
Value 0x211 delivered reports; 0x212 delivered timestamp-interleaved reports.
The old enum's exact semantic labels do not reliably describe the observed
RTL8922 behavior. Values 0..7, auto mode, both bands, segment choices and
ready choices were bounded-tested; only sparse packet-related reports were
observed. Counts can include reports already in flight across a mode change.
The official Windows 6102.24.143.0 image contains no direct 0x11460/0x15460
or old 0xce60/0xee60 constants. Its BB scope helper sets only the two BB bits.

Remaining technical requirement
Find and enable the hardware producer/routing that sends raw LA ADC/DFIR
records to the scope/PCIe RX DMA queue, or implement a card-internal transfer
from reserved LA SRAM into correctly formed DMA receive buffers. The PCIe
RX queue itself is functional; a host RX-ring size increase alone cannot
route raw LA samples into it. Internal DMA and DLE CPU enqueue helpers exist
in source, but their existence does not establish an arbitrary SRAM-to-host
copy path. No speculative host physical-address DMA was programmed.
Gapless receive remains unqualified. Realtek I/Q transmit testing remains
pending the user's receive qualification gate.
