Capture status and measurement notes

Continuous experimental 160 MS/s reception. Live retunes pause capture briefly and mark a gap; the driver stays loaded. Capture faults mark a gap and automatically reload the isolated driver; the browser reconnects at the last tuned center. Stop Demo ends reception. Transport has bounded validation; indefinite sample-level continuity remains unqualified.

The receiver continuously drains I/Q and targets 240 FFT frames per second. The browser batches incoming waterfall rows at its screen refresh rate. This is a spectrum view, not a complete recording. Text is painted by changing Pluto’s small cyclic multitone buffer about every 0.3 seconds; row changes briefly gate transmission. The nominal text canvas is 1 MHz wide; spectral skirts and hardware leakage can extend beyond it. The highlighted band is the programmed position, not an RF measurement. Oscillators are uncalibrated, and power is relative dBFS per FFT bin, not dBm.
