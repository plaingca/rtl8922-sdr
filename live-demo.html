Coverage and measurement notes

Free tuning programs the receive synthesizer below the Wi-Fi channel API. The channel selector remains a convenient preset list; direct frequency entry is not restricted to that list. Fine tuning uses a digital oscillator. Full output bandwidth near a tuning boundary is not guaranteed. A hardware retune restarts reception and inserts a separator in the waterfall. Display frames are snapshots from a continuously drained stream; this browser is not a full I/Q recorder. Power is relative to digital full scale, not calibrated dBm.

Pluto reference reception is verified at selected centers through 6.000 GHz. Higher 6 GHz centers tune and produce samples, but RF reception there is unqualified. Additional RF-qualified synthesizer encodings expose 2312–2382, 2500–2515 and 4900–5000 MHz. Gaps remain; full S/C-band coverage has not been achieved. The measured frequency offset is about −11 kHz at 2.4 GHz and −25 kHz near 6 GHz. The optional Pluto tone is transmitted only after Start / apply tone is pressed.
