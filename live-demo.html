Capture status and measurement notes

This demo uses experimental 40 MS/s reception at 2422–2462 MHz. A separate 15-minute raw transport test passed its loss counters; sample-level continuity remains unqualified. This CF32 display path reports its own interruptions above. Retunes create intentional gaps and a separator in the waterfall.

The receiver continuously drains I/Q; the browser shows short FFT snapshots, not a complete recording. Text is painted by changing Pluto’s small cyclic multitone buffer about every 0.3 seconds; row changes briefly gate transmission. The nominal text canvas is 1 MHz wide; spectral skirts and hardware leakage can extend beyond it. The highlighted band is the programmed position, not an RF measurement. Oscillators are uncalibrated, and power is relative dBFS per FFT bin, not dBm.
